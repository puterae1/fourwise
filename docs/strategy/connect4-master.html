The spine

Every move, in this order

Stop as soon as one fires. This replaces every other rule — the shapes and traps below are just instances of it.

Can I win right now?PLAY ITCheck your own board before hers. People lose won games looking for danger.

Can she win next move?BLOCK ITBoth ends of every row. All four diagonals. Every turn.

Does my move let her win on top of it?REJECTThrow the move away and test the next candidate. This is the mistake costing you games.

Can one move create two threats?PLAY ITGame over. Parity is irrelevant here — she can only block one. Traps 1 and 2.

Can I make one threat on my parity row she can't reach yet?PLAY IT, THEN GO QUIETFire once. Then stop attacking and let the clock run. Trap 3.

Nothing else?SAFEST CENTRE MOVEClosest to column 4 that opens nothing above it.

Offence

Three traps

Steps 4 and 5. Never build a three-in-a-row as a separate move — she just blocks it and you've shown your hand. Build two-disc gaps; the three appears on the move that wins.

Your threat sits on a row she can't reach yet. Her threat does too. Both columns are now dead to whoever would feed the other. Play safe, neutral moves. The board fills, her options vanish, and she is forced to play under yours.
